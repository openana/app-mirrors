We will be performing scheduled maintenance on our storage infrastructure on **February 25, 2025** between **02:00 and 06:00 UTC**.

## Impact

During this window:

- Some mirrors may experience brief sync interruptions
- Download speeds may be temporarily reduced
- The status page will show real-time updates

## What's Changing

We're upgrading our storage backend to provide:

- Faster sync speeds for large mirrors
- Improved reliability and redundancy
- Better support for growing mirror sizes

## After Maintenance

All mirrors will automatically resume normal operations. You can monitor the status on our [mirror list](/) page.

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience.
