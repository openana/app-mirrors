We're excited to announce the launch of our new mirror site! The site has been completely rebuilt with a modern design based on the mirrorz ecosystem.

## What's New

- **Modern Design**: Clean, dark-themed interface inspired by mirrorz
- **Real-time Status**: Mirror sync status updates automatically every 5 minutes
- **Search & Filter**: Find mirrors quickly with regex-enabled search
- **Download Browser**: Browse available downloads by category

## Available Mirrors

We currently provide mirrors for a wide range of Linux distributions, programming language package managers, and software repositories. Check the [mirror list](/) for the complete overview.
